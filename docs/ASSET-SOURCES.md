# Free Game Asset Sources for Sneakerheadz 3.0

A catalogue of candidate CC0 / public-domain / CC-BY art and audio for the
mini-games, assembled from web search.

> **Read this before you click anything.** Nothing here has been fetched. This
> development environment's network policy blocks every one of these domains —
> opengameart.org, kenney.nl, itch.io, freesound.org, pixabay.com, mixkit.co,
> fonts.google.com and the rest — so the URLs below come from search results,
> not from loading the pages. Treat every row as a **lead**, not a confirmed
> asset: the page may have moved, the pack may have been relicensed, and the
> licence column in particular is the thing most likely to be wrong.
>
> **Check the licence on the page itself before shipping anything.** That goes
> double for OpenGameArt, where the licence is per-submission (CC0, OGA-BY and
> CC-BY all live side by side and some items are dual-licensed), and for
> itch.io, where "free" and "CC0" are not the same claim.
>
> Rows that say "verify" or "check" are the ones search results were least
> clear about. Audit date: 2026-09-13.

## Rejected / Careful

Assets that appeared promising but have licensing issues or unclear terms:

| Asset/Source | Reason | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CraftPix.net Asset Packs | Non-commercial or restrictive licensing | CraftPix frequently uses "free for personal use only" or paid exclusive licenses. Requires careful per-asset license review. Avoid unless specifically CC0/CC-BY stated. |
| DaFont (broad browsing) | Mixed licensing, no CC0 guarantee | While DaFont hosts some OFL fonts, many are under unclear or personal-use-only licenses. Always read individual license before use. |
| Freesound.org (unfiltered) | Half the library is CC-BY or restrictive | Freesound has ~700k sounds, but only ~50% are CC0. Always filter by CC0 license tag. CC-BY tracks require attribution. |
| OpenGameArt.org (unfiltered) | Mix of CC0, OGA-BY, CC-BY; some unclear | OpenGameArt is gold-standard for free game art, but license varies by asset. Always verify license field on each item. OGA-BY and LPC licenses have specific terms. |
| Sonniss GDC Bundles | Royalty-free but prohibits AI/ML training | Sonniss audio is free for games/film but explicitly forbids AI training use. Check acceptable use case. |
| Incompetech | CC-BY (requires attribution) | Not CC0, but attribution is trivial (credit in game credits screen or README). Acceptable for commercial games if attribution given. |
